# 👋 Welcome to **Ada School's GitHub** — Where Digital Talent Is Born

We are an edtech organization on a mission to **make high-quality, employment-focused education accessible to everyone**. At Ada School, we believe that **your background should not define your future** — opportunity, learning, and impact should.

🚀 In a world rapidly shaped by technology, we train our students in the **most in-demand digital skills** so they can **build meaningful careers**. This GitHub space is a showcase of:

- 💻 Projects, labs, and real-world solutions built by our students
- 📦 Repositories with open educational resources
- 🔁 Collaborative initiatives with our partner community

---

## 📚 Our Methodology: ADA

> 🧠 “I hear and I forget, I see and I remember, I do and I understand.” — Confucius

Our educational model is based on the **ADA Methodology** – _Applied Digital Learning_. It combines self-learning, labs, projects, and collaboration to ensure deep, practical knowledge.

📘 Learn more → [Explore the ADA Methodology](https://github.com/ada-school/ada-methodology)
