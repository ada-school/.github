## 📚 Our Methodology: ADA

> 🧠 _"I hear and I forget, I see and I remember, I do and I understand."_ — Confucius

We believe in **learning by doing**. The **ADA Methodology** (_Applied Digital Apprenticeship_)
packages learning as **job-ready micro-credentials** (10–30h), each built from **Learning Atoms**
and sequenced through **4 phases** that follow the Confucius progression:

| 🙉 Self-Guided Intro | 🙈 Visual Exploration | 🙊 Applied Practice | 🐵 Collaboration & Reflection |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| *hear* | *see* | *do* | *share* |

## 🧬 ADA v2 — Skills that map to real jobs

Our methodology now deconstructs **any role an employer needs filled** into measurable learning:

- 🧠🛠️🌱 **KSA framework** — every skill split into **Knowledge · Skills · Abilities** so it is taught and assessed the right way.
- 🗺️ **Skills map → job matching** — badges write proven KSA levels into a learner's skills map, matched against a job's minimum bar.
- 🤖 **Gen AI authoring** — turn a job posting into a skills map, learning atoms, and rubrics — always with a **human-in-the-loop**.
- 🧩 **Reusable templates** — design a Micro Course for **any skill your team needs performed**.

🔗 Worked example: [Growth Mindset micro-credential](https://github.com/ada-school/ada-methodology/tree/main/examples/growth-mindset-micro-credential)
