## 📚 Our Methodology: ADA

> 🧠 _“I hear and I forget, I see and I remember, I do and I understand.”_ — Confucius

We believe in **learning by doing**, and our learning model, the **ADA Methodology** (_Applied Digital Apprenticeship_), is built around four stages:  
**Self-learning · Experimentation · Capstone Projects · Collaboration**

📘 Learn more → [Explore the ADA Methodology (EN)](https://github.com/ada-school/ada-methodology/blob/main/README.md)
